Why downtime matters for a small business

When your website is down, customers can't book, buy or find your phone number, and most won't try again later. Without monitoring, you usually find out from an annoyed customer, sometimes hours after the problem started.

What you get

  • Checks around the clock: every minute on Pro and Agency, every 2 minutes on Starter, every 5 minutes on Free.
  • Down and back-up emails sent to you, with the time it happened.
  • Response time tracking, so you can see when your site is getting slow.
  • Outage history (paid plans): when each problem started and how long it lasted. Useful when you need to hold your web host to account.
  • A public status page you can share with customers so they know you're on it.

What usually causes downtime

  • Your web hosting company having an outage
  • An expired domain name or security certificate
  • A website update or plugin that broke something
  • Too many visitors at once, or a server running out of resources

Uptraqr can't stop these from happening, but it makes sure you hear about them first, so you can call your web host or designer straight away.

Paid plans also include tamper watch, which checks for signs that someone has hacked your site, not just whether it's online.
