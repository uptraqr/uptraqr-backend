What the health check looks at

  • Security settings: whether your padlock (SSL certificate) is valid, visitors are always sent to the secure version, and your site uses the protective settings browsers understand.
  • Signs of hacking: "hacked by" messages, hidden spam links, disguised code, and pages that send visitors to other websites.
  • Speed: whether your homepage loads quickly and isn't too heavy for phones.
  • Google basics: page title, search description, main heading, works on phones, a sitemap, and whether Google is allowed to list your site.
  • Email protection: whether your domain has SPF and DMARC records, which stop scammers sending fake emails that look like they came from you.

How you get it

  1. See it on screen straight away. Type your website address on our homepage. No email, no account.
  2. Get it by email if you like. Enter just your email and we'll send you the full report, with one button to start watching that website for free.
  3. Every month on paid plans. Starter, Pro and Agency customers get a short health report for each of their websites on the 1st of every month, so new problems don't go unnoticed.
The health check only looks at the public parts of your website, the same things any visitor or search engine can see. It doesn't log in, change anything or try to break in.

What to do with your results

Start with the "Top things to fix" list. Each item says what's wrong in plain English and what to do about it. Many fixes take a few minutes in your website builder or hosting control panel. If someone else built your site, forward them the report: it lists exactly what to change.
