What Uptraqr watches for you

Three things that quietly cost small businesses customers. We check them around the clock so you don't have to.

Is it online?

We open your website every 5 minutes, just like a customer would. If it stops answering, you get an email and a text right away, and another when it's back.

Has someone tampered with it? New

Every 30 minutes we compare your page with its known-good version and look for the things attackers actually do:

  • "Hacked by…" messages or your page being replaced
  • New code from unknown websites that can steal card details
  • Contact or payment forms that now send to someone else
  • Hidden casino, pharmacy or adult spam links
  • Visitors being sent to a different website

Normal changes like today's date or a rotating banner are ignored. If you updated the page yourself, one tap tells us it was you.

Is the padlock about to expire?

When your security certificate (SSL) runs out, browsers show visitors a big "Not secure" warning. We remind you 30 days before it happens.

How tamper watch works

  1. We learn your page

    When you add your site, we take a snapshot of how it normally looks, and note which parts change on their own.

  2. We check every 30 minutes

    Each check compares your page with that snapshot and looks for attack signs.

  3. You hear about real problems

    Hacker signs trigger an email and a text. Smaller edits get an email asking if it was you.

  4. You confirm or fix

    Made the change yourself? Tap "Yes, I made this change." If not, our email tells you what to do next.
